## Deviations

Anything done differently from the procedure, and why. Write "None" if there were none.

1. **Payment was simulated for steps 11 to 13.** Paystack's hosted payment page sits behind a Cloudflare bot check that an automated browser cannot pass. Orders 005 and 006 were marked paid by a signed `charge.success` webhook sent to the UAT webhook route (HTTP 200). This proves the route, the paid state and the staff alert. It does not prove that Paystack's own servers reach the UAT webhook; that needs one order paid by hand on the Paystack page.
2. **UAT payment configuration was wrong at the start.** The Paystack settings held live keys, so Pay failed or offered no card option. The operator replaced them with test keys; later runs used the corrected setup.
3. **Step 3 evidence spans runs.** The empty-Pay error, the single field and the saved name were seen in the first run; the redirect to Paystack after Pay was seen in a later run, after the keys were corrected.
4. **Steps 15 to 18 BLOCKED on the operator's instruction** (no WhatsApp phone, templates pending), and the release is to continue to production. The reviewer must accept this or require a manual run. Production note: WhatsApp alerts to staff will not work until the production `staff_alert` template is approved and a real staff number is verified; the in-app bell, toast and Needs-attention panel do not depend on it.
5. **Rate limit.** The auth API allows 5 requests a minute per IP (#1042). Every navigation was paced about 24 s apart. The limit was not bypassed.
6. **Sound (step 11)** could not be checked by the agent.
7. **Mistakes by the agent** are listed in "Actions taken by the agent" (the production number and the Staff Pot Save).
